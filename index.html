Solar Panels

A solar panel is a device that converts sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic cells. Made up of silicon or other materials, these cells capture solar energy and generate direct current (DC) electricity, which is then converted into usable alternating current (AC) for homes or businesses. Solar panels are an eco-friendly, renewable energy solution that helps reduce reliance on fossil fuels and lowers electricity costs.
